Find your ski data

Open Ski History to see saved days. To keep a copy of all your days, go to Profile › Export Data. Choose JSON for full ski-day data, GPX for routes, or CSV for a spreadsheet summary.

To remove a day, open it in Ski History and choose Delete Ski Day.

Common questions

Why isn’t my route recording?

Allow location access for Summit in your iPhone’s Settings and enable Precise Location for better GPS detail. Start the recording in Summit before heading downhill. If GPS is slow to start, give your iPhone a clear view of the sky.

Can I record without cell service?

Yes. Start tracking in Summit with location access enabled. Your iPhone can record GPS offline. Fresh forecasts and uncached map data may need an internet connection.

Why is a resort forecast or map missing?

Check your data connection and reopen the resort. Coverage varies by resort, and online providers can be temporarily unavailable. If the issue persists, email the resort name and tell us which view you were using.

Can I export a single day?

Open the day in Ski History and use its export button. Check the destination before sharing a route file, since it can contain precise location data.
